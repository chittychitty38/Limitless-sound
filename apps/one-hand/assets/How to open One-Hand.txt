LIMITLESS ONE-HAND: FIRST TIME ON A MAC

1. Drag "Limitless One-Hand" onto the Applications folder next to it.

2. Open Applications and double-click Limitless One-Hand.
   One-Hand isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS may say it can't check the app.
   If it does:
     - Click Done (or OK).
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Limitless One-Hand.
     - Click Open Anyway again and enter your Mac password if asked.
   You only do this once.

3. One-Hand shows a welcome page with two switches to turn on:
   Accessibility and Input Monitoring. Each has a button that opens the right
   page in System Settings. The welcome page notices by itself when they're on.

4. Click the big button, or double-tap Shift, to switch one-hand mode on.
   Then press keys in the One-Hand window to practise, and open Logic Pro or
   Pro Tools to use them for real.

One-Hand keeps running in the menu bar (the 1H key icon) when you close its window.

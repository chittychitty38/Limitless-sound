Control Logic Pro, Pro Tools and the rest of your computer with your voice. Everything runs offline on your computer and works with any microphone.

**Mac:** download the .dmg for your Mac (Apple Silicon for M1 and later, Intel for older Macs), open it and drag Limitless Voice into Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open. A one-minute spoken setup walks you through the microphone and Accessibility permissions and a quick mic check.

**Windows:** run LimitlessVoiceSetup.exe. It installs for your user only, so no administrator password is needed. If SmartScreen warns you, choose More info > Run anyway.

Then click into your DAW and say "what can I say".

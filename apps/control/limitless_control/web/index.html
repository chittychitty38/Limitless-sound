Limitless Control

Play and record with an Xbox Adaptive Controller, switches or any gamepad.

Welcome. Let's get you playing.

  1. Connect your controller

    Plug the Xbox Adaptive Controller in with a USB-C cable. Or pair it: hold its small pair button until the Xbox light flashes fast, then choose it in your computer's Bluetooth settings.

    Waiting for a controller…

  2. Let Limitless Control press keys for you

    Your Mac asks you to allow this once. Choose the button, then switch on Limitless Control in the list that opens. You may need your Mac password.

    Not allowed yet.

    Already switched on and this still says not allowed?

  3. Try a button

    Press the big A button, or any switch.

    Nothing pressed yet.

  4. Open your music app

    Open Logic Pro, Pro Tools or Ableton Live. Limitless Control notices and switches to the right buttons for that app, and tells you out loud.

    Big A plays and stops. Hold the big B to record. Hold Menu to turn the controller off and on.

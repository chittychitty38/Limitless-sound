Play, record and mix with an **Xbox Adaptive Controller**, accessibility switches or any gamepad, in Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Ableton Live and the rest of your computer.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an M chip (M1 or later) | `LimitlessControl-mac-apple-silicon.dmg` |
| Older Mac with an Intel chip | `LimitlessControl-mac-intel.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `LimitlessControl-…-Windows-Setup.exe` |
| Linux | `LimitlessControl-linux.tar.gz` |

**Mac:** open the .dmg and drag Limitless Control onto Applications, then open it. The first time,
macOS blocks it because it isn't from the App Store yet: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**,
scroll down and choose **Open Anyway**. Then follow the welcome steps in your browser.

**Windows:** run the Setup file. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## What it does

- Big **A** plays and stops. **Hold** big **B** to record, so it never starts by accident.
- Follows the app in front: Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Ableton Live, or everyday computer use. It says the profile name out loud.
- **Layer** button for one-handed use, and an **on/off** switch: hold Menu.
- Tremor and bounce filtering, with Steady / Some tremor / Strong tremor presets.
- Menu bar icon on Mac. A settings page that works with VoiceOver, Narrator and zoom, with a live picture of your controller.
- Any button can also trigger the other Limitless Sound apps.

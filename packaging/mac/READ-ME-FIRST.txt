LIMITLESS SOUND FOR MAC

1. Double-click "Install Limitless Sound".
   The first time, macOS says it can't check the developer. Click Done, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to "Install Limitless Sound". This is needed once, for the whole suite.
2. The Mac installer opens. Click Continue, then Install, and type your Mac's
   password. Every app goes into Applications.
3. Limitless Sound opens in your web browser. From there you can open the apps
   you use and choose which ones open together.

What's inside
  Limitless Sound      Joins the apps into one suite. Runs quietly in the background.
  Limitless Voice      Voice commands for Logic Pro, Pro Tools and the whole computer.
  Limitless One-Hand   The keys under one hand become DAW commands.
  Limitless Zoom       Zoom the DAW or the screen by voice, one hand, dwell or a switch.
  Limitless Color      Recolors the screen for colorblind musicians.
  Limitless Control    Xbox Adaptive Controller, switches and gamepads as DAW commands.
  GazeStage            Play instruments and run the transport with your eyes or head.

The apps aren't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet. Signing will remove the
"Open Anyway" step entirely.

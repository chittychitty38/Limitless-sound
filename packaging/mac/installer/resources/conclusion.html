Limitless Sound is installed.

It’s opening in your web browser now, with two quick questions: how you like to play, and how things should look and sound. Then it opens the apps that fit you and starts them together from now on. It starts by itself when you sign in.

The first time you use an app, it asks for what it needs (the microphone, the camera, or permission to press keys for you) and says why.

Help and guides: chittychitty38.github.io/Limitless-sound

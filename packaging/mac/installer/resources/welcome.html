Limitless Sound

Music is for every body.

This puts the whole Limitless Sound suite in your Applications folder:

  • Limitless Voice: voice commands for Logic Pro, Pro Tools and the whole computer
  • Limitless One-Hand: the keys under one hand become DAW commands
  • Limitless Zoom: zoom the DAW or the screen by voice, one hand, dwell or a switch
  • Limitless Color: recolors the screen for colorblind musicians
  • Limitless Control: the Xbox Adaptive Controller, switches and gamepads as DAW commands
  • GazeStage: play instruments and run the transport with your eyes or head
  • Limitless Sound: joins them, so any input can trigger any app

It takes about a minute. Your Mac will ask for your password.

Everything works offline. Your voice and your camera never leave this Mac.

Free music software · Mac and Windows

Make music with the body you have. No one left behind.

Limitless Sound is a free suite of apps that lets musicians with disabilities play, record and mix in Logic Pro, Pro Tools and Ableton Live with their voice, one hand, their eyes, a single switch or an adaptive controller.

Early and honest about it. These apps were built in October 2026 and pass their automated tests, but they have not yet been tested by disabled musicians in real sessions. That is the next step, and the reason for this page. Expect rough edges, and please tell us what breaks.

Six apps. Any input can do anything.

Each app solves one barrier on its own. Together they share one action bus, so a button, a glance or a word can reach any of them. Everything runs offline on the musician’s own computer, with the mic, webcam, keyboard or controller they already own.

  • One hand
  • Eyes and head
  • A single switch
  • Foot pedal

  • Limitless Voice

    Run the DAW by speaking

    Say “go to bar thirty two”, “solo track” or “show the mixer” and it happens in Logic Pro, Pro Tools or anywhere on the computer. Recognition is fully offline: your voice never leaves the machine.

    For musicians who can’t use, or can’t always use, a mouse and keyboard.

    Download 0.3.1How it works

  • Limitless One-Hand

    The whole studio under one hand

    Switch on one-hand mode and the keys under your hand become the commands you use most: play, record, undo, split, mute, solo, zoom, save. Switch it off and the keyboard is normal again.

    For musicians with the use of one hand, left or right.

    Download 0.2.0How it works

  • Limitless Zoom

    See the session at any size

    Zoom the DAW or the whole screen by voice, a one-hand key layer, resting your pointer on big buttons, or a single switch. Every action is shown in large text and said out loud.

    For low-vision musicians, and anyone who can’t pinch, scroll or hold modifier keys.

    In the Mac suiteHow it works

  • Limitless Color

    A screen tuned to your eyes

    Describe what looks wrong (“the red meters look yellow”) and it recolors the whole screen, asking what you see now until it’s right. Pin Solo, Mute or the meters to an exact color.

    For colorblind musicians, in any app and any plug-in.

    In the Mac suiteHow it works

  • Limitless Control

    Switches and controllers become key commands

    Every button and switch jack on an Xbox Adaptive Controller, or any gamepad, becomes a DAW command. It follows the app in front, filters tremor and double hits, and puts Record on a hold so it never starts by accident.

    For musicians who play with switches, buttons, feet or limited movement.

    Download 0.2.1How it works

  • GazeStage

    Play with your eyes or head

    Play instruments and run the transport by looking, turning your head, making a short sound or pressing a switch. Uses the built-in camera or any webcam. Video never leaves the computer and nothing is recorded.

    For musicians who move mostly or only with their eyes and head.

    Download 0.3.1How it works

The Limitless Sound hub joins them

A small background app that works like a sidechain between the six. Each app still works on its own; with the hub, they work as one.

  • A controller button can zoom. A glance in GazeStage can turn on screen colors. A spoken phrase can start one-hand mode.
  • While recording, Voice only listens for “stop”, so singing in a take can’t trigger commands.
  • Text size, contrast and dwell time are set once for every app.
  • Built on an open protocol anyone can use: Limitless Link.

Opening it the first time

The apps aren’t signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so the computer asks once before it trusts them. This takes about a minute.

  1. Open the downloadDouble-click the .dmg file, then double-click Install Limitless Sound.
  2. macOS says it can’t check the developerThat’s expected. Click Done (or OK).
  3. Allow it onceOpen System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On older macOS you can instead right-click the installer and choose Open.
  4. You’re inEvery app is copied into Applications and Limitless Sound opens in your web browser, where you choose which apps start together.

On Windows

Download an app from its link above and run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, click More info › Run anyway. Windows installers are available for Voice, One-Hand, Control and GazeStage.

What you need

  • A Mac from roughly the last ten years (macOS 12 or newer for most apps), or Windows 10 or 11.
  • Whatever you already have: the built-in mic and camera, any USB webcam, a keyboard, switch or controller.
  • No account and no internet once installed. Free.

Where this is going

Software is the start. The goal is a program that puts working setups in the hands of disabled musicians, a song series that shows what they make, and an open standard so every instrument and plug-in fits them out of the box.

The plan

Limitless Sound: the 38x plan

  • Ongoing interviews and outreach with disabled musicians, as a ready-to-run program.
  • A sponsored pilot that fully kits out one or two artists in their own studio.
  • A guest-feature song series pairing them with players they admire, filmed as it happens.
  • Partners for gear, reamping, drums and accessible plug-ins.

The standard · draft 0.1

The Limitless Access Standard

MIDI let any keyboard play any synth. This does the same for access: any input a musician can use can drive any instrument, plug-in, amp modeler or recording app that carries the mark. Free to implement, with an open test kit.

  1. Level 1Connected
  2. Level 2Adaptive
  3. Level 3Hands-free
  4. Level 4Co-designed

A voluntary, compassionate standard written with disabled musicians. Not a legal accessibility certification.

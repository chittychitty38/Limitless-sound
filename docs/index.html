Early days

Honest about where this is. These apps were built in October 2026 and pass their automated tests, but they have not yet been tested by disabled musicians in real sessions. That’s the next step, and the reason for this page. Expect rough edges, and please tell us what breaks.

The suite

Six apps. Any input can do anything.

Each app takes down one barrier on its own. Together they share one action bus, so a button, a glance or a word can reach any of them. Everything runs on the musician’s own computer, with the mic, webcam, keyboard or controller they already own.

  • Voice

    Limitless Voice

    Run the DAW by speaking

    Say “go to bar thirty two”, “solo track” or “show the mixer” and it happens in Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Ableton Live, GarageBand, REAPER, FL Studio or anywhere on the computer. In English or Spanish, and a first preview in Brazilian Portuguese. Fully offline: your voice never leaves the machine.

    For musicians who can’t use, or can’t always use, a mouse and keyboard.

    Download0.4.4How it works

  • One hand

    Limitless One-Hand

    The whole studio under one hand

    Switch on one-hand mode and the keys under your hand become the commands you use most: play, record, undo, split, mute, solo, zoom, save. Switch it off and the keyboard is normal again.

    For musicians with the use of one hand, left or right.

    Download0.2.7How it works

  • Vision

    Limitless Zoom

    See the session at any size

    Zoom the DAW or the whole screen by voice (in English or Spanish), a one-hand key layer, resting your pointer on big buttons, or a single switch. Every action is shown in large text and said out loud.

    For low-vision musicians, and anyone who can’t pinch, scroll or hold modifier keys.

    Download0.1.10How it works

  • Color

    Limitless Color

    A screen tuned to your eyes

    Describe what looks wrong, in English or Spanish (“the red meters look yellow”), and it recolors the whole screen, asking what you see now until it’s right. Pin Solo, Mute or the meters to an exact color.

    For colorblind musicians, in any app and any plug-in.

    Download0.1.5How it works

  • Switches

    Limitless Control

    Switches become key commands

    Every button and switch jack on an Xbox Adaptive Controller, or any gamepad, becomes a DAW command. It follows the app in front, filters tremor and double hits, and puts Record on a hold so it never starts by accident.

    For musicians who play with switches, buttons, feet or limited movement.

    Download0.2.8How it works

  • Eyes

    GazeStage

    Play with your eyes or head

    Play instruments and run the transport by looking, turning your head, making a short sound or pressing a switch. A tone pad shapes your sound the same way, and can work a Line 6 Helix's expression pedals. Uses the built-in camera or any webcam. Video never leaves the computer and nothing is recorded.

    For musicians who move mostly or only with their eyes and head.

The hub

One suite, joined like a sidechain

A small background app links the six. Each still works on its own; with the hub, they work as one.

  • A controller button can zoom. A glance in GazeStage can turn on screen colors. A spoken phrase can start one-hand mode.
  • While recording, Voice only listens for “stop”, so singing in a take can’t trigger commands.
  • Text size, contrast and dwell time are set once for every app.
  • Built on an open protocol anyone can use: Limitless Link.

First open

One minute, once.

The apps aren’t signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so the computer asks once before it trusts them.

  1. Open the downloadDouble-click the installer you downloaded.
  2. macOS can’t check the developerThat’s expected. Click Done (or OK).
  3. Allow it onceOpen System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On older macOS you can instead right-click the installer and choose Open.
  4. You’re inThe Mac installer puts every app in Applications (it asks for your password). Then Limitless Sound opens in your browser, where you choose which apps start together.

On Windows

Download Limitless Sound for Windows and run it: it installs every app for you, with no administrator needed. If SmartScreen appears, click More info › Run anyway. Each app is listed in Settings › Apps under its own name.

What you need

  • A Mac from roughly the last ten years (macOS 12 or newer for most apps), or Windows 10 or 11.
  • Whatever you already have: the built-in mic and camera, any webcam, a keyboard, switch or controller.
  • No account, no internet once installed. Free.

Proof on fresh computers

Installed and tested on a fresh computer, every release.

With every release, a brand-new Mac (Apple silicon and Intel) and a Windows PC download Limitless Sound from this page, install it and open every app. Each one has to join the suite and answer every one of its commands within three seconds, and the suite has to work together: a command from one app reaching another, and every app following the suite into Spanish and back. If anything fails, it shows here.

Loading the latest results…

These computers have no microphone, camera or controller, so this proves the install, start-up, the suite and every command. Voices, faces and hands are tested by musicians. Every run, in full.

Real voices, not a test voice

Limitless Voice is tested against recordings of real people: about 200 people saying single words into their own phones and laptops, and an hour of 40 people reading aloud. It hears 91% of the words it listens for, and acts on “stop” about two-thirds of a second after you say it. In that hour of talk it fired no command with Logic Pro in front, and no stray word or sentence ever made it cut, paste or delete. Limitless Zoom zoomed 4 times in the same hour. How it’s measured.

Where this is going

Software is the start.

The goal is a program that puts working setups in the hands of disabled musicians, a song series that shows what they make, and an open standard so every instrument and plug-in fits them out of the box.

The plan

Limitless Sound: the 38x plan

  • Ongoing interviews and outreach with disabled musicians, as a ready-to-run program.
  • A sponsored pilot that fully kits out one or two artists in their own studio.
  • A guest-feature song series pairing them with players they admire, filmed as it happens.
  • Partners for gear, reamping, drums and accessible plug-ins.

The standard · draft 0.1

The Limitless Access Standard

MIDI let any keyboard play any synth. This does the same for access: any input a musician can use can drive any instrument, plug-in, amp modeler or recording app that carries the mark. Free to implement, with an open test kit.

  1. Level 1Connected
  2. Level 2Adaptive
  3. Level 3Hands-free
  4. Level 4Co-designed

A voluntary, compassionate standard written with disabled musicians. Not a legal accessibility certification.
